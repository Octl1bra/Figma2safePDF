FIGMA PLUGIN · PDF / PPTX THAT OPEN EVERYWHERE
Figma2safePDF
What you see is what they get.
Figma’s built-in export swaps fonts, drops effects and shifts layouts. This plugin renders every frame as one flat image, so the file opens pixel-identical in Acrobat, PowerPoint, Keynote, WeChat or on a phone.
